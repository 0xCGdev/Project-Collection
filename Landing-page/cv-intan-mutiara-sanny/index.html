OUR Story

A family workshop built on craftsmanship.

CV Intan Mutiara Sanny is an Indonesian manufacturer of handcrafted home décor, creating unique products from wood, stone, glass, bamboo, branches, rattan, and other natural materials. Combining traditional craftsmanship with modern production standards, we supply wholesalers, retailers, and interior brands across international markets.

Every piece is handcrafted by skilled artisans, carefully shaped, finished, inspected, and packed to meet export-quality standards. From decorative accents to functional home accessories, our products are designed to bring natural beauty into homes around the world.
